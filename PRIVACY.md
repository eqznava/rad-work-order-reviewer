# Privacy

Rad Work Order Reviewer is a Forge app that runs on Atlassian's platform.

- The app has read-only access: it reads the Jira issue you ask it to
  review and the reference card pages in the Confluence space `RPREF`.
- The app's code does not store, copy or send that content anywhere. It
  has no database, no external service and no analytics.
- It writes nothing to Jira or Confluence.
- The conversation with the agent is handled by Atlassian Rovo under
  Atlassian's own privacy policy: https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy
